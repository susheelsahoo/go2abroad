Courses

FIND THE RIGHT LEVEL

Undergraduate to PhD, we cover every level.

Not sure whether a diploma, a Master's or a full research program is right for you? Here's a quick breakdown of every course level we help students apply for — and what to expect from each.

Undergraduate

Bachelor's Degree

3–4 year full-time programs across Business, Engineering, Computer Science, Arts and more — ideal for students right after Class 12.

  • Direct entry after Class 12
  • Foundation / pathway options available
  • Co-op & internship pathways
  • Popular in Canada, UK, Australia, USA
Postgraduate

Master's Degree

1–2 year specialised programs (MS/MA/MSc) to deepen expertise and unlock higher-paying, skilled-migration-eligible roles.

  • Thesis & non-thesis tracks
  • Strong PR / PGWP pathways
  • STEM & Humanities specialisations
  • Popular in Germany, Canada, UK, Australia
Management

MBA & Management

Full-time, executive or online MBA programs for working professionals and career-switchers aiming for leadership roles.

  • 1–2 year formats available
  • GMAT / GRE waiver options
  • Strong global alumni networks
  • Popular in USA, UK, Canada, Singapore
Doctorate

PhD & Research

Funded research programs with stipends — ideal for students pursuing academia or advanced research careers.

  • Fully-funded stipend options
  • Research supervisor matching
  • Publication & conference support
  • Popular in Germany, USA, UK, Australia
Diploma

Diploma & Certificate

Short, skill-focused programs (6 months – 2 years) offering fast, affordable entry into the global job market.

  • Lower tuition & faster completion
  • Strong for vocational & trade careers
  • Pathway to Bachelor's / PR
  • Popular in Canada, Australia, Ireland
Foundation

English Language Programs

Foundation and intensive English programs, linked to IELTS/TOEFL/PTE, to meet university admission requirements.

  • Conditional admission pathways
  • Academic & General training tracks
  • Score-improvement guarantee programs
  • Available before any destination country
